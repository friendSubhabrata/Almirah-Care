Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit your workshop directly?

Yes, absolutely! You can visit our workshop at Medigem, Sodepur Road, Madhyamgram, Kolkata 700129 anytime between 9:00 AM and 8:00 PM (Monday to Sunday) to inspect paint quality, shade cards, and sample works.

Can you spray paint the almirah at my home terrace?

Minor touch-ups and lock repairs are done on-site. For complete high-gloss Duco or PU spray painting, we recommend workshop painting to avoid paint mist, chemical odor, and to achieve an oven/booth dust-free finish.

How long will the new paint last?

Our automotive-grade Duco and PU paint coats last 8–10 years under normal indoor use and come with a 3-Year Anti-Peeling Warranty.
